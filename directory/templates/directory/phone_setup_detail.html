Connect the phone to home Wi-Fi

  1. Put the WP816 on its charger and open Quick Network Configuration on the phone. Choose the option to configure it with a mobile browser.
  2. On your iPhone or other mobile device, join the Wi-Fi hotspot shown on the WP816 screen.
  3. Wait for the Wi-Fi setup page to open. Choose your home network, enter its Wi-Fi password, and finish setup.
  4. After the WP816 says Network connection success, reconnect your mobile device to its usual network and return here.
